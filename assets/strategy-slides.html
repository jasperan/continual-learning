The Problem

Catastrophic
Forgetting

When neural networks learn new information through gradient descent, they overwrite existing knowledge. A model fine-tuned on medical text forgets how to write code. One trained on legal docs loses conversational ability.

Overwriting
New gradients destroy old weight patterns
No Isolation
All weights shared between old and new tasks
Our Goal
Learn at inference time, preserve existing knowledge

TF-IDF Gating — Protects general-purpose neurons by masking their gradients during backprop. Only document-specific neurons receive full updates. Threshold: 0.3, calibrated on 2,000 samples.

Strategy Comparison

Strategy Approach Learn Generate Forgetting Requires
TTT-E2E Weight modification ~secs standard LOW GPU 24GB+
